Security and privacy

Your mail stays yours. Vega never opens Gmail, never reads a message and cannot get the password of any account. No program on your laptop can count Google accounts from outside: Google does not allow it.
Nothing runs from the internet. Windows only opens what you click. Her helper listens on this laptop alone, so another machine cannot send it orders.
No key, no leak. With the built-in brain, nothing you type is sent anywhere. If you connect an AI model, only that question goes to that service, and only while you use it.
The helper is powerful on purpose. It can open apps, change volume and shut down, so keep the black window for yourself and close it when you finish.
Read-only. It reads Windows settings and counts saved browser sign-ins. It never opens a password store, never reads a mail box and changes nothing.
Vega can tell you how to see which accounts are signed in, but she cannot list them for you. To see every Google account on this laptop: open myaccount.google.com and check the account switcher, or open Gmail and click your picture. To see every Microsoft account: account.microsoft.com.
To see every site that remembers you: in Chrome, Settings > Autofill and passwords > Password Manager. That list is the real answer to "where am I logged in", and only you can open it.
Change the password of the account you are worried about, from that account's own site, and turn on two-step login. If someone has been in your accounts, sign out of all devices from that account's security page. If money or a threat is involved, tell a parent or a trusted adult today and keep the messages.
