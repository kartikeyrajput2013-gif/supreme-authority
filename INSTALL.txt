SOPHIA - PUT IT ON YOUR LAPTOP IN ONE STEP
==========================================

I cannot reach your laptop from here. No program on my side can open your
folders or install anything on your computer. So this file does the installing
for you: you download it, you double-click one thing, and it copies itself
into place.

WHAT YOU DO (about one minute)
------------------------------

1. Download Sophia-Updated.zip from GitHub.
   Open it, and click "Extract All" (or right-click > Extract All).

2. Inside the extracted folder, double-click:

        INSTALL_SOPHIA.bat

3. A black window opens and asks you where Sophia should live.
   It looks for your delivery app folder by itself and offers it to you.
   Press 1 to accept, or 2 to pick another folder.

4. It then:
     - copies assistant.html, sophia_helper.py and Start_Sophia.bat
     - keeps a backup of your old assistant.html
     - puts a shortcut called "Sophia" on your Desktop

5. It asks "Start Sophia now? [Y/n]". Press Enter.

That is it. From now on, just double-click Sophia on the Desktop.

If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box
--------------------------------------------------------
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". That warning appears for any file
downloaded from the internet; it is not a virus warning. If you prefer, you
can instead right-click Install_Sophia.ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell".

WHAT NEEDS PYTHON
-----------------
Full laptop control (volume, brightness, closing apps, the app list) needs
Python. If Python is not on the laptop, Sophia still starts in browser mode:
websites, maths, memory, feelings, knowledge, quiz and games all work, and
Windows will still open apps from her buttons. To get the full version,
install Python from python.org and tick "Add python.exe to PATH", then run
Start_Sophia.bat again.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Your old page is kept as assistant-old-<date>.html in the same folder.
Rename it back to assistant.html and you are exactly where you were before.
The installer never deletes your delivery app, your photos or anything else.

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS VERSION
---------------------------
- A card called "Every app on this laptop": every app Windows knows about,
  with a search box. One click opens it.
- Open buttons no longer do nothing. Every open either works, opens a tab,
  or tells you in words why it could not and what to press instead.
- The launcher buttons and the "Open" links now work in browser mode too.
- Feelings: 16 kinds, with gentle answers and real helpline numbers.
- Knowledge: 30 topics, about 190 notes, 96 quiz questions, 60 quick facts,
  12 riddles, everything offline.

WHAT IS STILL HONEST ABOUT HER
------------------------------
- She reads the words you type or say. She cannot see your face, cannot hear
  your tone, and is not a doctor.
- She is a program, not a person.
- A web page cannot start programs by itself; only Windows can. That is why
  the app list and the buttons exist, and why the .bat matters.
