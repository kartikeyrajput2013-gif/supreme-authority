SOPHIA UPDATE - HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR LAPTOP
============================================

I cannot open your laptop from here, so this is the one part only you can do.
It takes about one minute and it is just copying one file.

STEP 1  Download the new file
    Sophia-Updated.zip is in this folder on GitHub.
    Click it, then click the download button.
    Windows saves it in Downloads.

STEP 2  Unzip it
    Right-click the ZIP, choose "Extract All", then Extract.
    You will see a folder with these files:
        assistant.html      <- the new Sophia
        sophia_helper.py    <- lets her control the laptop
        Start_Sophia.bat    <- double-click this to start her
        README.md           <- the full guide

STEP 3  Keep the old one as a backup
    Go to the folder where Sophia lives now.
    It is the same folder as your delivery app, probably called
    "DELIVERY APP STUFF".
    Right-click the old assistant.html, choose Rename, and name it
    assistant-old.html
    Do not delete it yet.

STEP 4  Copy the new one in
    Copy assistant.html (and sophia_helper.py and Start_Sophia.bat)
    from the extracted folder into the folder from Step 3.
    Windows will ask "There is already a file with this name".
    Choose "Replace the file in the destination".

STEP 5  Start her
    Double-click Start_Sophia.bat as usual.
    Look for the new things:
        - a "How are you feeling right now?" card with faces
        - a "Things I can teach without internet" shelf
        - in Settings, "How your week has felt"

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
    Delete the new assistant.html, rename assistant-old.html back to
    assistant.html, and you are exactly where you were before.
    Nothing else on your laptop is changed by this update.

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS VERSION
    - 13 feeling families (sad, lonely, worried, angry, tired, bored,
      confused, ashamed, grieving, unwell, hungry, can't sleep, and happy)
      and a gentle answer for each one.
    - A crisis answer: if you say you want to die, she stops and gives
      real helpline numbers (Tele-MANAS 14416, Childline 1098, 112) and
      asks you to talk to an adult today.
    - 30 knowledge topics, about 190 notes, 96 quiz questions, 60 quick
      facts and 12 riddles that work with no internet at all.
    - A mood history you can see and clear any time.

WHAT IS STILL HONEST ABOUT HER
    - She reads the words you type or say. She cannot see your face or
      hear your tone, and she is not a doctor.
    - She is a program, not a person.
